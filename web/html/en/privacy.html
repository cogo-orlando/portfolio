Privacy policy

This site collects as little data as possible: no cookies, no ads, no third-party analytics. Here is exactly what is processed, why, and for how long.

Data controller

Orlando Cogo, publisher of this site — Orlando.cogo.pro@gmail.com

Data processed

WhatWhyLegal basisRetention
Visit log: anonymized IP address (last block removed, e.g. 203.0.113.0), page requested, date, response code Running the site, detecting outages Legitimate interest A few days, depending on the hosting provider's log retention

None of this data is sold, shared for advertising, or used for profiling.

Cookies

This site sets no cookies, which is why there is no consent banner.

Fonts are hosted on the site itself: no request is sent to Google or any other font service.

Service providers

  • Render (application hosting) — United States
  • Cloudflare (domain, HTTPS, attack protection) — receives your IP address to route and filter traffic
  • Formspree (delivers contact form messages)
  • Open-Meteo: the weather on the Status page is requested directly by your browser, which therefore sends your IP address to this service

Some of these providers may process data outside the European Union, notably in the United States. These transfers rely on the safeguards provided by the GDPR (EU–US Data Privacy Framework or standard contractual clauses, depending on the provider).

Security

Every connection is encrypted (HTTPS). The site has no database, and visitor IP addresses are anonymized before being written to the logs. The protections are detailed on the Infrastructure page.

Your rights

You can request access to your data, its correction or deletion, restriction of its processing, or object to its processing. Write to Orlando.cogo.pro@gmail.com: you will receive an answer within one month.

Because visitor IP addresses are anonymized, it is usually not possible to find the logs of a specific person.

If you believe your rights are not respected, you can lodge a complaint with the French data protection authority, the CNIL, or the authority of your country.
