Why cybersecurity

"

To me, cybersecurity is truly the future. With the rise of artificial intelligence and all the new technologies, the IT field keeps changing — and that is exactly what excites me. I'm convinced that understanding these changes has become essential to protect systems properly.

After working in the aerospace industry, I realized I wanted to grow in a field that never stops moving. Cybersecurity is exactly that: a sector that never sleeps, where every day brings new challenges and new threats to analyze and counter.

Working in highly structured industrial environments like Airbus taught me rigor and method — two essential qualities in security. Today I bring that rigor to every project: automated tests, static security analysis with gosec, automated CI/CD, monitoring in production.
